# Self-Evolution Decision Log

Append-only 演化决策日志。每次 Human Evolution Approval 后追加一条记录。

## Log Format

```text
YYYY-MM-DD | {decision} | {pattern_signature}
- Change IDs: {list of change ids}
- Action: {rule-update / skill-update / validator-update}
- Target: {file path}
- Reason: {decision rationale}
```
